### cra.art14 — CRA Art. 14 — BLOCKING — answered: no
**Requirement.** Is there a runbook for reporting an actively exploited vulnerability, with a named owner and an out-of-hours path?
**Why it matters.** 24 h early warning, 72 h notification, 14 days to the final report — no size exemption, no weekend pause. Live since 11 Sep 2026.
**Evidence to produce.** runbook with CSIRT/ENISA steps, owner, escalation, and the pre-filled 24 h field set

### cra.sbom — CRA Art. 13(18), Annex I Part 2(1) — BLOCKING — answered: no
**Requirement.** Is there a machine-readable SBOM identifying top-level dependencies?
**Why it matters.** Named in the vulnerability-handling requirements; also the input every questionnaire asks for.
**Evidence to produce.** sbom.json / CycloneDX or SPDX file, generated by CI, with a date

### cra.techdoc — CRA Annex VII — BLOCKING — answered: partial
**Requirement.** Is the technical documentation set assembled — design, risk assessment, SBOM, test reports, declaration of conformity?
**Why it matters.** Without it the self-assessment cannot be defended to a market surveillance authority.
**Evidence to produce.** Annex VII indexed pack with document versions and dates

### rodo.breach — RODO Art. 33/34; formularz UODO — BLOCKING — answered: partial
**Requirement.** Is there a breach procedure with a 72 h path to Prezes UODO (biznes.gov.pl or ePUAP UODO/SkrytkaESP) and a subject-notification decision?
**Why it matters.** Clock starts at 'stwierdzenie' — sufficient certainty that a personal-data breach occurred, not at first rumour. Successive notification is allowed if marked 'zgłoszenie wstępne'.
**Evidence to produce.** procedure, UODO form template, decision log, ePUAP/biznes.gov.pl path tested

### rodo.ewidencja — RODO Art. 33(5) — BLOCKING — answered: no
**Requirement.** Is an internal register of ALL personal-data breaches kept, including those not notified to UODO?
**Why it matters.** Art. 33(5) covers every Art. 4(12) breach. Low-risk events stay in the register even when they are not notified.
**Evidence to produce.** ewidencja naruszeń with circumstances, data involved, effects, remediation

## Clocks that apply

### CRA (EU 2024/2847)
- 24 h — early warning to the coordinating CSIRT and ENISA on awareness of an actively exploited vulnerability (CRA Art. 14)
- 72 h — vulnerability notification (Art. 14(2)(b))
- 14 days — final report once a corrective measure is available (Art. 14(2)(c))
- Reporting live since 11 Sep 2026; full CRA (essential requirements, CE marking) from 11 Dec 2027

### uKSC (Dz.U. 2026 poz. 252) — Polish NIS2
- 3 Apr 2026 — uKSC amendment in force; incident reporting applies immediately
- 3 Oct 2026 — S46 / Wykaz KSC self-registration deadline (wykaz-ksc.gov.pl)
- 24 h / 72 h / 1 month — incident report to the competent CSIRT (NASK / GOV / MON) via S46
- 3 Apr 2027 — Chapter 3 SZBI measures must be operational
- 3 Apr 2028 — first mandatory cybersecurity audit (essential entities); penalty moratorium ends
- 14 days — report any change to S46-declared data

### RODO + u.o.d.o. (Prezes UODO)
- 72 h — zgłoszenie naruszenia do Prezesa UODO after stwierdzenie (Art. 33); biznes.gov.pl or ePUAP UODO/SkrytkaESP
- without undue delay — notify data subjects where high risk (Art. 34)
- Always — ewidencja naruszeń for every Art. 4(12) breach, notified or not (Art. 33(5))
- Video monitoring: 3-month cap unless needed as evidence (u.o.d.o.)

### KSeF (FA(3), obowiązek 2026)
- 1 Feb 2026 — receiving structured invoices obligatory for all; issuing obligatory for large (>200 mln PLN 2024)
- 1 Apr 2026 — issuing obligatory for remaining VAT payers (except micro <10k PLN/month gross)
- 1 Jan 2027 — issuing obligatory for micro; KSeF sanctions in force
- Payment clocks at many counterparties run from the KSeF ID, not the invoice date

### Prawo komunikacji elektronicznej (UKE)
- Notice before recording — no grace period
- PKE Art. 398 — prior consent for unsolicited electronic marketing
- UKE register — if you are a telco; write down the conclusion either way

## Hand-off

- Ready to sign the EU declaration themselves → Annex VII pack, then stop.
- Needs a notified body (Class II, critical, or Class I without harmonised standards) → NANDO list.
- Needs ISO 27001 → PCA/IAF-accredited body. No commission.
- KSC registration / CSIRT path → S46 (wykaz-ksc.gov.pl); CSIRT NASK default for private sector.
- Personal-data breach → Prezes UODO via biznes.gov.pl or ePUAP UODO/SkrytkaESP.
- Legal opinion on scope or liability → a lawyer. We do not write those.
